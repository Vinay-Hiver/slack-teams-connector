# Modal

A box that opens over the page to ask for a decision or a bit of input. It has a header with a title and a close button, a white body, and the actions on the right. It comes in widths of 400px and 500px.

## Title

The title can stand on its own or have a small icon before it. Use the icon for serious actions such as deleting or discarding.

<Canvas of={ModalStories.TitleIcon} />

## Confirm

A title, a line of text and two buttons: Cancel and the main action.

## Destructive

An icon before the title and a red button, for things that cannot be undone.
