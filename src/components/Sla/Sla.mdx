# SLA

Shows where the first response time (FRT) or resolution time (RT) of a conversation stands. A small pill and a wider alert bar, in four statuses: upcoming (grey), due (orange), overdue (red) and done (green).

## Pill

A small label with a lightning bolt and FRT or RT.

<Canvas of={SlaStories.PillStatuses} />

## Alert

A bar with the bolt, a message and an arrow. The message reads "First response…" for FRT and "Resolution…" for RT.
