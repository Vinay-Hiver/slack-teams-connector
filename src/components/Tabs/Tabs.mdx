# Tabs

The row of tabs that switches between views of the same content. Clicking a tab shows a small ripple.

## Outlined

Underline tabs on a thin line. The selected tab is bold with a dark underline. Each tab can show a count.

<Canvas of={TabsStories.Outlined} />

## Filled

A pill selector for two or three options. The selected tab is a white pill on a grey track.
