# Tooltip

A short label that appears when someone hovers or focuses an element, to explain what it does. It appears after a short pause of 300ms, so it does not flash while the pointer is just passing by, and then fades in over 200ms. Keep it to a few words.

## Placement

It can appear above, below, left or right of the element.
